Frequently Asked Questions

What types of trucks do you service?
We service prime movers, rigid trucks, tilt trays, tipper trucks, crane trucks and most commercial vehicle configurations. Call us if you're unsure.
Do you service diesel engines?
Yes. Diesel engine servicing and diagnostics are a core part of what we do. We handle common rail diesel systems, turbo servicing and DPF maintenance.
Can you do roadside repairs?
In some situations we can arrange mobile support. Contact us to discuss your circumstances.
Do you provide loaner vehicles?
We don't operate a loan vehicle fleet, but we can work with you on scheduling to minimise disruption to your operation.
