Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Introduction

Ballarat Commercial Service Centre (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when using our website contact forms, including your name, phone number and email address. We may also collect information about your vehicle and the services you require.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry or booking request
  • To provide the automotive and commercial vehicle services you have requested

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, provided they agree to keep your information confidential.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.
