Heavy Vehicles Need More Than Just a Quick Fix

Heavy vehicles face greater loads, longer distances, and more wear than the average car. When something isn't right, you feel it quickly, and if you leave it too long, minor issues can turn into significant downtime. Our team is trained to handle the added demands of commercial and diesel vehicles.

We don't cut corners or treat your truck like a hatchback. Instead, we conduct thorough inspections, provide transparent reporting, and perform quality repairs using parts and methods that meet heavy vehicle standards. Whether it's brakes not biting properly, steering pulling, or a check engine light you can't clear, we'll find the problem and fix it correctly the first time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What heavy vehicles can you service?
We service a wide range, including prime movers, rigids, delivery vans, buses, trailers and diesel utes. Call us if you're unsure — chances are we can help.
Do you offer fleet servicing for local businesses?
Yes. We can set up regular maintenance schedules for small to mid-size fleets. We'll work with you to minimise vehicle downtime and ensure compliance.
Can I bring my vehicle in for a pre-trip check?
Absolutely. We offer pre-trip inspections for long-haul or seasonal work to reduce the chance of breakdowns on the road.
Do you offer same-day service?
We aim for same-day turnaround where possible, especially for standard repairs and services. We'll let you know once you book what the timeline will be.

Yes, we are licensed to conduct roadworthy inspections and issue certificates for heavy and commercial vehicles.
